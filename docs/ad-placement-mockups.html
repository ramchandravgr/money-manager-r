Money Inbox — small ad placement mockups

Wireframes for non–full-screen ads (banners, small native units, corner tiles). Blue dashed boxes are ad slots. Not implemented in the app — planning reference only. Sizes shown are proportional; real units are often 320×50 banner, 300×250 medium rectangle, or adaptive anchored above the nav bar.

Proposed ad zone A Above bottom nav (all main tabs) B Below period / summary strip C Inline in scroll lists D List footer (end of scroll) E Small corner overlay F Settings sub-screens only (optional)

Placements to avoid (finance + trust)

  • SMS approval / pending transaction review (widgets + in-app queue)
  • Add / edit transaction, loan, asset, or recurring forms
  • Backup & restore, Google Drive, LAN sync pairing
  • Web Manage login and any screen showing balances during a sensitive action
  • Full-screen interstitials on cold start or after recording a payment
