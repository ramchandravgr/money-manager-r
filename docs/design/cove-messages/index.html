The daily edition.

Cove reads like a paper you open, not a list you scroll. The newest message from a person is the headline. Your reply is the next line of a letter. Codes, ads, and suspicious texts are inserts in that paper: a ticket, the classifieds, a sealed envelope.

The idea

Every major SMS app is a contact list that opens into balloons. Cove uses a different object. You are reading today’s edition of your own messages, and you write back in the margin.

The message is the page The latest unread line from a person is set in headline type. You read it before you tap anything.
A letter, not a bubble A conversation is one manuscript. Their words sit on the rule. Yours sit in italic in the margin.
Machines are inserts A code is a ticket. Offers are classifieds. A risky text arrives sealed, with the reason on the outside.
Reply without entering The front page has a line under the headline. Most replies never open the letter.

Front page

There is no row of faces. The newest human message is the headline, with a reply line under it. Everyone else is an index: a name, then the line, with a clay star in the margin when they are unread.

  • Search pulls down from the masthead and covers the edition.
  • The ticket at the foot is the only machine on this page. Its count is codes, not a mixed badge.
  • A new message starts from a blank sheet, reached by writing a name into the reply line when it is empty.

The letter

This is the conversation. One scrolling manuscript, oldest toward the top, the latest line just above the pen. Their words are roman on the red rule. Yours are italic, moss, and indented, with a small status under the line: Sent, Delivered, or a plain failure such as “Personal has no signal.”

  • A day change is a centered label, Yesterday or Today, not a new bubble group.
  • A photo is a print pasted into the paragraph, with the caption on the next line.
  • The dock is the pen. While a line is being written, the nib travels the rule and lifts at the end. The letters stay the normal manuscript type. Attachments sit behind a long-press on the stamp.
  • Long-press a line for copy, forward, remind, or remove.

The ticket

One code fills the screen, set like an admission stub. Tear-off copies the digits. The next ticket is already underneath, so the stack has a count you can feel.

Classifieds

Promotions are the back page, in two narrow columns, delivered once in the morning. They never become headlines.

Telegrams

Banks, deliveries, and bookings are short strips that end in STOP. A held message is not opened here. It is handed over as a sealed envelope.

Sealed

The reason is printed on the outside. Breaking the seal shows the original text. The judgment is made on the phone.

Stationery

A blank ruled sheet. The SIM is a postage stamp in the corner: Personal or Work. The letter begins at To.

Colophon

Settings read as the staff box of a paper: edition, who may interrupt you, where the archive lives, who is refused, and which other apps hold a subscription.

What still has to work

The object is new. The duties of a messenger stay. The left column is the daily product. The middle is depth. The right is what this shape makes possible.

Duties

A real messenger

Required, expressed in the edition.

Default SMS roleSend, receive, and post the system notification.
Letters and groupsText, photo, video, audio, contact, location. A group is a letter with several hands.
Two stampsEach SIM is a named stamp. The letter remembers which stamp it uses.
NotificationsA person can interrupt. The ticket and the classifieds cannot, except the morning edition.
SearchPull the masthead. The index is on the phone.
OrganizePin a letter to the top of the index, archive it, mute it, mark it unread.
BlockRefused senders never make the edition.
Schedule and undoA line can wait until evening. A short hold lets you pull a line back.
Lock and archiveThe paper opens with the device lock. A backup is a bound copy on the device.
Large screensFront page on the left, the open letter on the right.

What others skipped

These need the paper. A themed chat list cannot host them.

Headline replyAnswer the newest person from the front page.
Margin voicesTwo speakers, told by position and italic, with no balloons.
Ticket stackCodes as tear-off stubs with an expiry, one at a time.
Back pageOffers in columns, one morning delivery.
Sealed postA risky text stays closed until you break the seal. The reason is on the outside.
Telegram stripMachine alerts in a few words, separate from letters.
Subscription receiptAnother app may take a column you name. The colophon lists the fields and the cancel.

Build path

Each phase is a paper you can already use. Later phases add inserts and crafts. They do not turn the edition back into a chat list.

Phase 1EditionFront page, letters, stamps, notifications, search, block, lock, on-device archive.
Phase 2InsertsTickets, classifieds, telegrams, sealed post with on-device reasons.
Phase 3CraftScheduled lines, undo, folded letters, private names, reminders, wide layout, watch.
Phase 4SubscriptionsA colophon receipt, then an optional Money Inbox column of fields you tick.
